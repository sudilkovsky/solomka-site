Privacy Policy

Solomka: 2 Player Games. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Solomka collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no purchases and no server. Nothing about you, your child or how you play ever leaves your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Which golf courses you played recently, so the next game picks a fresh one.
  • Game settings, such as ball speed in golf, if you change them.

Both are saved on the iPhone and used only by the game.

Scores and matches are not kept after a game ends.

Permissions

None. Solomka never asks for the camera, the microphone, your photos, your location or your contacts, and doesn't use them. (The game engine's code mentions the camera, microphone and photos, so their descriptions are in the app's settings file, but the app never asks for them.)

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no ads and no crash reporters, and it never connects to the internet.

Children

Solomka is made for parents and children to play together. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the recent courses and settings with it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
